# Tassie Trip – January 2027 (DRAFT)

Advanced trip, limited places. Participants must be signed off vertical cavers with
sufficient caving experience and be open to additional pre-trip training.
Participants can join for individual phases.

**Change from host's itinerary:** skip the Expedition phase (22–26 Jan) and run a
canyoning block instead, around Cradle Mountain or another region.

| Date (Jan 2027) | Phase | Notes |

the north. Canyoning is 23–26 (4 days), allowing for 1 rest/weather-spare day.

## Phases people can join
- A: Junee Florentine only (15–21)
- B: Canyoning only (22–26)
- C: Mole Creek only (27–31)
- Any combination. Consecutive phases share vehicles/gear.

water temperature (wetsuits needed), access tracks, group size limits.
