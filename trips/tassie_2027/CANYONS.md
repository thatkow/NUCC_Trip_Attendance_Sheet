# Cradle Mountain area canyons – for review

Source: web search only (operator sites + one club trip report). Grades, abseil counts
and access are NOT verified for a private group. Treat as a shortlist to research.

| Canyon | What we know | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **Dove Canyon** | Signature canyon. Narrow ravine, jumps, slides, abseils. Full day ~6–8 h guided. | operator, UNSWOC report |
| **Machinery Creek / Canyon** | Higher and wider than Dove, series of waterfalls, ~30 m drop, ~6 abseils, ~5 h (older Qantas article). | operator, Qantas |
| **Lost World Canyon** | Shorter, easier; marketed to families (age 8+). Half-day version. | operator |
| **Minnow Falls** (Mt Roland, near Gowrie Park) | Not a Cradle canyon. Highest multi-tier waterfall in Tas (~227 m) on Mt Roland's eastern escarpment. 2018 exploratory descent report. Escarpment walking route reported closed (rope ladders unsafe, 2019 forum). RopeWiki's "Minnow Canyon" is Arizona, not this. | fatcanyoners, bushwalk forum |
| **Phoenix Gorge** | Listed on operator site; no details found. | operator |

Season: summer (about Nov–Mar), so January is fine, but check flows.

Notes
- Everything found is run by one commercial operator (Cradle Mountain Canyons, departs from
  the Cradle Mountain Visitor Centre). Their canyons may be on PWS-permitted/commercial-only
  access. Need to ask whether a private group can descend them and what permits apply.
- Nothing found for Mersey/Forth/Mole Creek canyons. Not evidence that none exist; ask local
  clubs (e.g. Tasmanian Caverneering Club, UNSWOC who have visited) and check canyoning
  guidebooks/Wildcanyons-type resources.
- Fallback: book a guided trip (Dove and/or Machinery) if private access isn't allowed.

## Reference links per canyon
- Dove Canyon: https://ropewiki.com/Dove_Canyon (3C1 I, max rappel ~49 ft / 15 m, ~2 h; commercially run, contact operator to coordinate)
- Machinery Creek: https://ropewiki.com/Machinery_Creek (3B II, 6 abseils, max ~92 ft / 28 m, 3–5 h; minor heavy-metal pollution, bring water)
- Lost World Canyon: no RopeWiki page found; https://www.discovertasmania.com.au/things-to-do/tours/cradlemountaincanyons/the-lost-world-canyon
- Phoenix Gorge: no references found
