# Camping near Cradle Mountain (23–26 Jan) – for review

Source: one web search only. Prices, availability, group-size limits and booking rules NOT checked.
January is peak season, so book early.

| Option | What we know | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **Discovery Holiday Park Cradle Mountain** | Main option: tent and campervan sites, amenities, camp kitchens. Described as walking distance to the Cradle Mountain Visitor Centre (canyon operator departure point). One source says ~15 km from the park entrance near Moina, so confirm location. | [Hipcamp](https://www.hipcamp.com/en-AU/national-park/australia/tasmania/cradle-mountain-lake-saint-clair/all), [Camplify](https://www.camplify.com.au/blog/best-caravan-parks-cradle-mountain) |
| **Cradle Mountain Fishery & Camping** (2369 Cradle Mountain Rd, Moina) | Rustic: BBQs, power, toilets, RV-friendly. | [Camptoo](https://www.camptoo.com.au/campsites/tas/cradle-mountain-fishery-camping) |
| In-park camping | Reportedly very limited: a couple of car-accessible campgrounds and hike-in sites (some reserved for Overland Track). Confirm with PWS. | [Tasmania.com](https://tasmania.com/things-to-do/national-parks/cradle-mountain-lake-st-clair-national-park/) |
| Lake St Clair | Not suitable: it is at the far south end of the park, hours away. | |
